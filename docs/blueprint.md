# **App Name**: Purrfect Whiskers

## Core Features:

- AI Pet Compatibility Matcher: A tool that uses reasoning to analyze a user's home life and activity levels to recommend specific cats or dogs currently awaiting adoption.
- Filterable Rescue Directory: A functional UI for browsing available pets with real-time filters for species, breed, age, and location in the UK.
- Gift Aid Donation Portal: Tiered donation interface with integrated UK Gift Aid checkbox logic and a monthly toggle.
- Rescue Journey Showcase: Story-driven UI cards that spotlight individual pet transformations from rescue to rehoming.
- Three-Step Adoption Visualizer: An interactive step-by-step roadmap guiding potential owners through the application, home check, and matching stages.
- Volunteer Onboarding System: A modular registration form for community members to sign up for fostering, shelter assistance, or local event support.
- Impact Analytics Dashboard: Visual representation of the NGO’s success rates, such as pets rehomed and funds raised for medical care.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Warm Orange (#F28C52), evoking compassion and energetic optimism for new beginnings.
- Background Color: Soft Cream (#FFF6ED), providing a gentle and approachable canvas for emotional imagery.
- Accent Color: Sage Green (#4E7A5D) for critical calls-to-action (CTAs) and success notifications, signifying growth and nature.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' (Bold/Medium). Body font: 'Inter' (Regular). Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Warm, earthy-brown (#6B4F3A) stroke icons featuring organic shapes to represent the pet/human connection.
- 60-30-10 distribution using clean modular grids and mobile-first navigation headers.
- Smooth fade-ins and scale transitions for pet cards to enhance the emotional storytelling of rescue stories.
